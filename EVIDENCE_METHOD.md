# Evidence method

Quantitative food composition requires a traceable source URL, publisher, year, exact table/food identifier, unit, edible portion, preparation and evidence quality. Values from the FAO/University of Dhaka table are averages for same or similar descriptions and are expressed per 100 g edible portion of fresh weight. A matching record stays specific to its table description, cultivar and raw/cooked state; it must not be generalized to other forms. Bracketed or ambiguous components are omitted. Serving values are not derived from the table unless an exact local serving weight is supplied.

The verified 2013 source is the [Food Composition Table for Bangladesh PDF](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/templates/food_composition/documents/FCT_10_2_14_final_version.pdf). Exact component identifiers and values are stored in each food's `nutrients[].evidence`; values without an exact match remain `null`, with status `not-verified` or `not-reported` for a matched entry. The displayed values for selected fruits remain limited evidence because table records can aggregate same/similar descriptions and sample counts vary.

The [Bangladesh National Dietary Guidelines 2025 PDF](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/searo/bangladesh/national-dietary-guidelines-for-bangladesh.pdf) could not be extracted because the source exceeded the research tool's content-size limit. No claims depend on its unreviewed contents. The current [WHO healthy diet page](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet) and [WHO Five Keys to Safer Food page](https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-safe-food-handling/five-key-to-safer-food) were checked and are linked only for general principles. BFSA is linked as an official starting portal; specific regulation text remains unverified.

No nutrient percentage daily values, health outcomes or disease-treatment claims are inferred from food composition data. Unknown values are displayed as “তথ্য যাচাই চলছে / Data not yet verified.”
