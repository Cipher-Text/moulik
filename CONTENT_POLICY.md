# Content policy

Moulik is independent consumer education, not a seller directory, certification body, medical service or marketplace. Never imply endorsement, purity, fraud, diagnosis, cure or clinical review without evidence. A food's composition varies by cultivar, edible portion, storage and preparation.

Health content must distinguish population-level education from diagnosed-condition care. Include individualized-care cautions where needed. Only mark clinician-reviewed when a named clinician and review record exist. User-generated health data is not collected. Correct errors promptly and preserve source context.
